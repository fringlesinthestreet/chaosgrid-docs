# ChaosGrid — Decision history

The code repo's `docs/DECISIONS.md` states each decision as it stands, with no history (D79). This page keeps the history: the code repo's whole decision log as it stood on 2026-11-09, below, and after it every change since, newest first. A change to a code decision adds an entry under Changes on a lead branch here, next to its CHANGELOG entry: the number, what the row said before, what it says now and why.

## Changes

### 2026-11-09 — Resources as cards of the deck

Resources join the main deck as static permanents on the grid (D85), searches and mana replace resource draws (D86), and the hand becomes a window (D87). The rows below were rewritten; what they said:

- **D26** said: **A main deck holds at most 4 copies of a card, and 1 of a Unique card.** Units, artifacts and enchantments may be Unique (`"unique": true`); every Leader is Unique, and spells, resources and Chaos cards never are. No effect makes a copy of a Unique card itself. The resource deck has no copy limit
- **D57** said: **Resource pools are public, and each lies behind its seat's grid.** Every seat sees every seat's pool: its resources and which are rested. The pool is outside the grid: it has no cells, nothing is placed in it, and row, column, area and grid effects never reach it. The sizes of every main deck, resource deck and discard pile stay public. During Plan each seat sees the other seats as they were when Plan opened, before its draws, pools and sizes included, so a seat's draws show with its plan. Every change to a pool is an event that everyone sees once it's public: `resource.played` when a resource enters a pool, and a rest. The battlefield draws each seat's pool and piles (card backs for the two decks) behind its grid, from the model, with the sizes from the seat's latest snapshot, and a click on a resource shows its card
- **D60** said: **Players start with no resources and draw one a round.** The setup draws 5 cards and no resources (`startingResources` 0), and every Plan draws 1 resource (`resourcesPerRound` 1), so a player has as many resources as rounds played, plus what ramp adds
- **D64** said: **Costs are paid only with floating mana: nothing but `restForMana` rests a resource for mana.** The plan action `restForMana` rests one unrested, unattached resource and adds one mana that pays what the resource gives. Casting, leveling and Plan abilities spend floating mana, and only floating mana: core never rests a resource for mana on its own, so an action the floating mana can't pay is skipped as `unaffordable` and changes nothing. Bots rest for mana in their plans too, right before each action that pays, with the resources core's payment preferences pick. A snapshot's `usable` still counts the resources the seat could rest. Floating mana lasts until the plans lock; what's left then is lost. A resource rested for its Rest ability gives no mana
- **D69** said: **A resource card can be Unique: a resource deck holds one copy of it.** Prism, Harvest Cycle, Old Library and the five colored Harvest Cycles are Unique. A card's `unique` flag means the same in both decks; the main deck's limits are otherwise D26's
- **D71** said: **The grid is the planning surface.** During Plan the player acts on the battlefield: a click on a pool resource in its strip rests it for mana or uses its Rest ability, a click on a squad or a permanent opens the card view with its actions beside the card (Level up, Plan abilities), and the hand lies in a tray at the bottom with the Leader's slot at its left, empty while the Leader stands on the grid. Every seat's strip shows its main deck, its resource deck, its hand as a pile of card backs with its size, its pool, its discard pile and its void. The tray has no Resources or Piles section, the planning panel no Leader or Plan section, and there is no Zones panel; a refused action shows on its card. The grid never hides behind the tray: the camera keeps it in the free part of the screen. Squads show their art without their names, and the TopBar's points show each seat in its color
- **D72** said: **The snapshot says what the floating mana pays now.** Each hand card, level-up and Plan ability in a seat's snapshot carries `payable`: whether the seat's floating mana pays its cost as the plan stands, as `plan.checked`'s snapshot shows it. `usable` (D64) says whether the floating mana and the resources the seat could still rest pay it together. The UI shows an action the floating mana can't pay inactive, with its cost, and nothing else disables it (D29)
- **D83** said: **Floating mana can come from an ability, and a card with such an ability is Unique and costs at least 2.** The `addMana` effect, on a utility Plan ability, adds one floating mana that pays a pip of a color it gives or a generic pip, like a resource rested for mana. It is lost when the plans lock, like all floating mana, and the snapshot lists it beside the floating resources. A card with an `addMana` ability is Unique, and its casting cost's mana value is 2 or more

### 2026-11-09 — Current rows only, and balance v2

- **The page states only what holds now (D79).** Removed: the superseded rows D7, D12, D25, D27, D31, D39, D48, D49, D55, D58, D61 and D68, and the withdrawn rows D14 to D17, D36 and D53, which WORKFLOW.md keeps. Every other row lost its "(Replaces Dn.)" and "As Dn:" prefixes, its dates and its playtest stories, with no change of rule; their wording on this date is in the log below.
- **D60** said "so round 1 has the 2 resources its Plan draws, as every later round adds 2". Now every Plan draws one resource (`resourcesPerRound` 1), so round N has N before ramp. Why: the human found mana grew too fast and cards too expensive early.
- **D26 and D67** listed traps among the cards that can be Unique and the permanents that stand again; traps are gone (D81).
- **D65 and D76** are removed: one card a round spawned Chaos-factor times (the round number in tier 1, round − 4 in tier 2, 2 in tier 3), capped at the round number of squads per player, Extra Chaos included. D80 replaces both. Why: after round 5 the Chaos was unbearable.
- **New:** D80 (a condition, an Emblem and a unit card a round), D81 (no traps), D82 (Chaos off), D83 (ability mana, Unique, at least 2), D84 (one level-up a round).
- **The other docs.** PROTOCOL.md › Versioning keeps the rule and the current version; its table of what each version changed, v1 to v38, moved to [Protocol versions](#protocol-versions) below. The packages/cards README's "Changes from the earlier draft format" moved to [The card format's draft](#the-card-formats-draft). Both are history, which D79 keeps here.

## The card format's draft

How the effect DSL differs from the card format first drafted in RULES.md, as the packages/cards README had it on 2026-11-09.

The DSL merges synonyms, so core has fewer effects to run:

| Draft                                | DSL                                          | Card                        |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| `setStat`                            | `buff` with `set`                            | Vask, Siege Beacon, Drought |
| `reduceCost`                         | `buff` of the player stat `firstLevelUpCost` | Ancient Cache               |
| `preventDamage`                      | `buff` of `damageTaken` with `"factor": 0`   | —                           |
| `forbidCast` (`"cardType": "spell"`) | `setRule` `noSpells`                         | Fog of War                  |
| `removeHandSizeLimit`                | `setRule` `noHandSizeLimit`                  | Old Library                 |
| `drawResource`                       | `draw` with `"what": "resource"`             | Stoke the Embers            |
| `redirect`                           | the script `mirror_snare`                    | Mirror Snare                |
| an ability with only a `script`      | `"effect": "script"`                         | Raider Pack, Mirror Snare   |

And renames a few things to say them one way:

| Draft                                          | DSL                                                                             | Card                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| `"rounds": 2`, `"duration": "nextRound"`       | `"duration": { "nextRounds": n }`                                               | Supply Drop, Siege Colossus                  |
| no duration on an Emblem buff                  | `"duration": "game"`                                                            | Crown Shard, Ancient Cache                   |
| `ownUnit`                                      | `chosenOwnUnit`                                                                 | Ember Vent, Overclock, Relocate              |
| `opponentRow`, `opponentTraps`, `emptyOwnCell` | `chosenOpponentRow`, `chosenOpponentTraps`, `chosenEmptyOwnCell`                | Flash Flood, Scorch Line, Undertow, Relocate |
| `"inArea": true`                               | `"zone": "sourceArea"`                                                          | Flare Mine                                   |
| `"at": "cleanup"`                              | `"delay": "cleanup"`                                                            | Tide Pool                                    |
| `draw` with no target                          | `"target": "owner"`                                                             | Harvest Cycle, Tide Pool, Undertow           |
| `grantKeyword` with `amount`                   | `value`                                                                         | Barnacle Plate                               |
| `"rests": true`                                | dropped: RULES.md rests every free-standing permanent after an activated effect | Tide Clock                                   |
